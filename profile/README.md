## What DeployU is

DeployU is built by [Lightbooks Technologies](https://deployu.ai/about) for engineering students and early-career engineers in India and the US who need proof of skill, not a playlist of videos. Every lab runs in a real environment (a real terminal, a real AWS account, a real application that has to run), and every step is checked against what you actually built.

**Four things you can do on DeployU today**

| Pillar | What it is | Where |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Hands-on labs** | 117 verified labs across Linux & Git, Cloud (AWS), DevOps (Docker, Kubernetes, Terraform, CI/CD), Full Stack and AI & Data, plus 17 live sandboxes (6 real AWS accounts, 11 container playgrounds) | [deployu.ai/courses](https://deployu.ai/courses) · [playgrounds](https://deployu.ai/playgrounds) |
| **DSA and placement prep** | 549 problems (404 DSA across 16 patterns, 145 AI & Data), graded by hidden tests in the browser, grouped by pattern and by 100 companies, with a readiness score that only counts solved problems | [deployu.ai/placements](https://deployu.ai/placements) |
| **AI mock interviews** | An AI interviewer asks about the code you actually wrote and about your resume, with scored feedback per answer | [deployu.ai/placements](https://deployu.ai/placements) |
| **Resume builder** | Builds a fresher resume from what you completed on the platform, with AI editing that is not allowed to invent anything, and PDF export | Inside Placements |

**One flagship project.** [CineList](https://deployu.ai/projects): a React, Node, Express and MongoDB app you build in 8 verified modules (about 7 hours) on a live dev server with a real IDE, then deploy with Docker. Plus 5 AWS project labs: RAG with Amazon Bedrock, identity verification with Rekognition, ALB + Auto Scaling, VPC, S3 + CloudFront.

```
 1. Open a lab            2. Get a real environment       3. Build it                 4. Get it verified
 Linux, Docker, K8s,      A browser IDE and terminal,     Run the commands, write     The verifier inspects the
 Terraform, AWS, AI,      a container, or a real AWS      the code, deploy the        container, resource or test
 full stack, placements   account. Nothing to install.    resource.                   and tells you which check
                          No cloud bill for you.                                      failed and why.
```

Every completed course issues a certificate with a public verification link listing the labs that passed.

## Pricing

Creating an account is free. Running labs, projects, sandboxes and placement prep needs a yearly plan, billed in your local currency. The cloud bill is always ours.

| Plan | Who it is for | Price (India) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Starter | First- and second-year students: foundation labs, real sandboxes, certificates | ₹18,000 / year |
| Professional | Job seekers: advanced labs (Kubernetes, Terraform, CI/CD, AI on Bedrock), CineList and AWS projects, placement prep and mock interviews, AI tutor | ₹24,000 / year |
| Advanced | Expert-level labs as they ship, priority support | ₹36,000 / year |

Students at partner colleges do not pay; they sign in at their college's DeployU address. Details and the refund policy: [deployu.ai/pricing](https://deployu.ai/pricing).

## For colleges

DeployU runs as your college's own lab platform: your subdomain, your roster, your semester map. Students get per-student real environments; faculty get a live class monitor, verified lab reports and step-failure hotspots instead of screenshots; the institution gets CO attainment and the CO–PO matrix exportable for NBA/NAAC, and placement-readiness by cohort. No cloud bill. [Request a demo](https://deployu.ai/colleges).
